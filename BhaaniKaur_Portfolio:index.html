About Me

Turning research
into strategy

I'm a rising junior at UC Berkeley's Haas School of Business studying Business Administration with a focus on strategy, brand consulting, and consumer insights.

I've always been drawn to understanding people — how they discover products, build trust, and form communities. That curiosity drives my approach to consulting: I combine rigorous primary research with sharp strategic thinking to help brands identify opportunities and act on them clearly.

Through BCEC (Business Careers in Entertainment) at Haas, I've led end-to-end consulting engagements for consumer brands across health, beauty, and entertainment — running surveys, focus groups, competitive analysis, and delivering actionable recommendations to brand leadership. I'm also gaining cross-functional experience as a PM at Parkyresource.org, where I own the product roadmap and translate user research into execution.

I'm drawn to work that sits at the intersection of data, storytelling, and execution — where a well-researched insight becomes a decision that actually moves a brand forward.
